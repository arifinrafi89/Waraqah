# Waraqah

A student book marketplace: new books from vendors, second-hand books between students, short book posts, and a reading assistant.

## Language

**Benefit tag**:
An admin-curated label on a **Book** saying whether it is **Beneficial** or **Non-beneficial**. Every book carries exactly one of the two.
_Avoid_: rating, category

**Beneficial**:
A book the admin judges useful to the reader's faith or learning: Islamic, science and coding titles.
_Avoid_: halal, approved

**Non-beneficial**:
A book the admin does not endorse for the general reader, such as fictional stories. The admin's choice is final, even for non-fiction. Whether a book benefits a particular reader depends on their intention and grounding: scholars of tafsir consulted the Torah and the Bible for context, yet such books are Non-beneficial in general and can harm a reader who is not careful.
_Avoid_: haram, bad, banned

**Benefit filter**:
The home-page choice between all books, only **Beneficial** books, or only **Non-beneficial** books.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Is a novel with a moral lesson **Beneficial**?"
> **Domain expert:** "Only if the admin tags it so. The **Benefit tag** is a curation decision, not something the app works out."
